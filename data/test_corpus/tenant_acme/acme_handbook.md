# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook (separate-tenant test document)

This document belongs to a **different** collection than the Nimbus corpus. It exists to
verify that collections are isolated and that answers about one collection never pull
facts from another.

## Company facts

- Acme Corp's company mascot is a **red panda named Pip**.
- The monthly all-hands meeting is held on the **first Monday** of each month at 10:00.
- Head office is in **Portland, Oregon**.

## Time off

- Full-time employees receive **25 days** of paid vacation per year.
- Vacation does not roll over; unused days are paid out in December.
- Sick leave is separate and uncapped.

## Equipment

- New engineers choose either a 14" or 16" laptop.
- Home-office stipend is **$500 per year**.
