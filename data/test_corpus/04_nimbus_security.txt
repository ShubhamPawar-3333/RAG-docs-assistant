Nimbus Analytics - Security and Compliance

ENCRYPTION
All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Data in transit is protected
with TLS 1.3; older TLS versions are refused at the load balancer.

COMPLIANCE AND CERTIFICATIONS
- SOC 2 Type II - report available under NDA from the Trust portal.
- GDPR - Nimbus acts as a data processor; a Data Processing Addendum is signed on request.
- CCPA - supported for all California-based customers.
- HIPAA - available on the Enterprise plan only, and requires a signed Business
  Associate Agreement (BAA). HIPAA is NOT available on the Free or Pro plans.

AUTHENTICATION AND ACCESS
- Single sign-on via SAML 2.0 is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans.
- SCIM user provisioning is available on Pro and Enterprise.
- Two-factor authentication (TOTP) is available on every plan, including Free.
- Session tokens expire after 30 minutes of inactivity by default.

DATA HANDLING
- Hosting is on AWS in the us-east-1 (North Virginia) and eu-west-1 (Ireland) regions.
- When a workspace is deleted, its data is retained for 90 days and then permanently
  purged from primary storage and backups.
- Customers can request an immediate hard delete by contacting security@nimbusanalytics.com.
- A current list of sub-processors is published at nimbusanalytics.com/subprocessors.

TESTING
- A third-party penetration test is performed once per year.
- Automated dependency and container scanning runs on every deployment.
- Bug bounty submissions are accepted at security@nimbusanalytics.com.
