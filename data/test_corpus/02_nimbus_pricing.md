# Nimbus Analytics — Plans and Pricing

Nimbus is sold on three plans. Prices below are per workspace and are quoted in US dollars.

| Plan       | Monthly price | Included events / month | Seats     | Data retention        |
|------------|---------------|-------------------------|-----------|-----------------------|
| Free       | $0            | 100,000                 | 3         | 30 days               |
| Pro        | $99           | 5,000,000               | 15        | 12 months             |
| Enterprise | Custom quote  | Unlimited               | Unlimited | Configurable, up to 5 years |

## Billing details

- **Annual billing** is discounted **20%** compared with paying month to month.
- On the **Pro** plan, event **overage** is billed at **$0.50 per additional 10,000 events**.
- The **Free** plan has no overage: once 100,000 events are reached in a calendar month,
  further events are dropped until the next month.
- Plan changes take effect immediately; downgrades are prorated as account credit.
- Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period. Payment is due within 30 days.

## What is not included

- The Free plan does **not** include SSO, the Export API, or email support.
- Phone support is available on Enterprise only.
- A 14-day free trial of Pro is available to every new workspace (see the onboarding guide).
