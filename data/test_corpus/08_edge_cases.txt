LONG PARAGRAPH (chunk-boundary test)
The following block of text exists so that this document spans multiple chunks and the
retriever must select the right one. Nimbus Analytics began as an internal tool built to
answer a single question: which onboarding steps actually correlate with a team sticking
around after ninety days. That narrow origin still shapes the product. Funnels came first,
then cohorts, then dashboards to hold them together, and only much later the Insight Engine
that watches those metrics automatically. Every feature added since has been weighed against
the same test - does it help a team see cause and effect in how their product is used, or
is it just another chart. The pricing model follows from the same idea: teams should be
able to start for free, prove value on real data, and only pay once their event volume
shows the product is being used in earnest. This paragraph carries no unique facts; it is
padding whose only job is to push the needle fact above into its own retrievable chunk.
